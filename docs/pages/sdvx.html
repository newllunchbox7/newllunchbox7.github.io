What is SDVX?

A little info

Sound Voltex, (abriviated as SDVX) is a arcade rhythm game developed by Konami and released on January 18, 2012 with their first installment, Sound Voltex Booth.

SDVX is played with 4 large BT buttons and 2 small FX buttons along with 2 knobs on the top right and top left sides of the controller.
The BT buttons (BT-A to BT-D) are used to hit the small white notes appear in the four main lanes.
the FX buttons (FX-L and FX-R) are used to hit the larger orange notes which take up the two lower lanes on the left and right.
The lasers (VOL-L and VOL-R) are the red and blue lasers on each side of your screen. VOL-L is blue and VOL-R is red. (these can be changed in settings however) You hit the lasers by turining the knob in the direction the laser is going.

Now that your settings are changed, you now get to download and try out some skins.
The easiest way to get skins is to go to skins.osuck.net Here you can find almost every OSU! skin. Look around and try to find one that looks good to you.

Once you find a skin you like, open the skin's page then head to the download tab. From there, it should give you multiple versions of the skin. (or sometimes only one) From there you will need to click on the download button that says "osk" (which is OSU!'s skin file) After you click it it will give you options on where to download the skin from. You can pick any of them. Once the .osk is done downloading, open your file manager and open it. (double click) It should automatically import into OSU!

You can change your skin by opening the settings then navigating to the 'skins' tab on the left then clicking the skin dropdown.
Do note that not all skins support all gamemodes and you will have to check which modes it supports on osuck.
Some skins might have a tag before the name such as <NM1>,<NM2>, or <DT> typically this refers to the type of skin it is. For example NM1, 2, or 3 stand for No Mod 1, 2, or 3 which refers to playing OSU! with no mods enabled such as: Double Time(DT), Hardrock(HR), or Hidden(HD) And you probably can figure out that <DT> refers to a skin that you would use when playing Double Time. There can also be other tags, but those usually are the skinner's name or a player's name.
