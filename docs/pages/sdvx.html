Intro To Sound Voltex

Starting with the basics.

Sound Voltex, (abriviated as SDVX) is a arcade rhythm game developed by Konami and released on January 18, 2012 with their first installment, Sound Voltex Booth.

SDVX is played with 4 large BT buttons and 2 small FX buttons along with 2 knobs on the top right and top left sides of the controller.
The BT buttons (BT-A to BT-D) are used to hit the small white notes appear in the four main lanes.
the FX buttons (FX-L and FX-R) are used to hit the larger orange notes which take up the two lower lanes on the left and right.
The lasers (VOL-L and VOL-R) are the red and blue lasers on each side of your screen. VOL-L is blue and VOL-R is red. (these can be changed in settings however) You hit the lasers by turining the knob in the direction the laser is going.

Where to play

Konasute, simulators, or the arcade?

Konasute

Konami's offical way of playing SDVX at home

I've never used Konasute and don't really plan to, so some of my information may be incorrect. Feel free to check sdvx.org's page on Konasute. for more info

Konasute is a monthly subscription service by Konami that lets you play SDVX at home while syncing your gameplay data and stats between the arcade and your computer. The UI is based off of Sound Voltex: Exceed Gear which came out on Febuary 17, 2021 and was replaced by Sound Voltex: ∇ (nabla) on December 24, 2025.
Konasute's monthly subscription costs 1,628 yen per month (about $10.31 USD as of 10/8/26) with additional song packs avalable for purchase.
Konasute supports up to 120hz refresh rate and Training mode.
(locked behind Konasute Premium Pass which adds an extra 500 yen per month)

(do note that Konasute only works on windows)
